Docs2Wallet

Support · Updated October 14, 2026

Docs2Wallet turns a ticket or document you already have into a personal Apple Wallet pass. It needs iOS 17 or later. If something here doesn’t answer your question, email arison.dev@gmail.com.

Importing a ticket

Import a screenshot, a PDF, pasted text or a ticket link in the app, or use the share sheet from another app to send up to three images, one file, text or a link to Docs2Wallet. The material is read on your iPhone to suggest the pass fields.

Checking the pass before it’s signed

Every field appears on the review screen before anything is sent. Docs2Wallet leaves out personal details such as ID numbers, card numbers and dates of birth automatically, and leaves attendee names off unless you turn on Show name on the pass. The filter can miss things, so read the fields before you confirm.

The code won’t scan

Raise the screen brightness and hold the pass steady. If the venue still can’t scan it, open the ticket link on the back of the pass to show the original. A pass from Docs2Wallet is a personal copy; the venue’s own ticket is what counts.

Free passes and the unlock

Your first passes each year are free. A one-time purchase unlocks more. If you reinstall or move to a new iPhone, open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

“App Attest” errors

Docs2Wallet uses Apple’s App Attest to prove each signing request comes from the real app. It needs a physical iPhone and the App Store version of the app. If authentication fails, try again; if it keeps failing, delete and reinstall the app.

Removing a pass

Open the pass in Wallet, open its details, and tap Remove Pass. Docs2Wallet does not keep your passes after you quit it, and the signing service does not store them. The privacy policy has the details.
