Gear Studio

Privacy Policy · Updated October 14, 2026

This policy describes how the Gear Studio app for iPhone and iPad handles information. Gear Studio is published by Arison. It is a gear design tool that works without an account, and the developer does not run a server for it.

What Gear Studio stores and where

Gear Studio does not collect personal information, and it does not send what you make to the developer. It stores only what you create in the app, so that you can come back to it:

  • Designs: the gears and gear sets you save, with the names you give them and when they were created and changed.
  • Custom library items: materials and processes you add with Gear Studio Pro.
  • Settings: your units, default material and process, pressure angle, tolerance profiles, display options, and which version of the terms you accepted.

Designs and custom library items are kept in the app’s storage on your device and synced through your private iCloud database, as described below. Settings and tolerance profiles stay on the device and are not synced.

Gear Studio asks for no device permissions. It does not use your camera, photos, location, contacts or microphone, and it does not read other apps’ data.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, Gear Studio syncs your designs and custom library items to the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your account. The developer cannot see or read it.

To keep these changes up to date, iCloud may send Gear Studio silent background notifications when another of your devices changes data. Gear Studio does not show you notifications and does not ask for permission to send them.

If iCloud is turned off for Gear Studio, or you are not signed in, your designs stay on this device only. Apple’s handling of iCloud is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Gear Studio Pro is sold through the App Store as a one-time purchase or an annual subscription. Apple handles the payment. The developer does not receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

To decide whether Pro is unlocked, Gear Studio asks the App Store on your device which purchases your account owns. It reads the product, and for the subscription its expiry or revocation date, and keeps that only while the app is running. It does not store its own copy and does not send it anywhere. Restore purchases asks the App Store to resync your purchases.

Exporting and sharing

You can export a design as STL, 3MF, OBJ or DXF, or as a PDF or CSV spec sheet. An export contains the design’s geometry and the names you gave the design and its gears. A spec sheet also lists the material and process, including custom ones, the tolerances and any warnings. It is prepared in the app’s temporary folder on your device, which iOS clears over time.

An export leaves your device only when you choose to: through the share sheet, or by saving it to a location in the Files app. Where it goes after that is up to you and the app or service you send it to.

Third parties

Gear Studio contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It does not sell or share your information.

The only services involved are Apple’s: iCloud for sync, and the App Store for purchases. If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may share crash reports for Gear Studio with the developer. You can turn that off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Gear Studio includes the open-source Manifold geometry library, which runs on your device and makes no network connections.

Retention and deletion

Your designs and settings stay until you delete them.

  • Delete a design, a custom library item or a tolerance profile in the app. Deleting a design removes it from this device and, when sync is on, from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device.
  • Designs already synced to iCloud stay in your iCloud until you delete them in the app on another device, or remove Gear Studio’s data in your iCloud storage settings.

Because the developer holds none of your designs or settings, there is nothing for the developer to keep or delete on its side.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn iCloud sync on or off for Gear Studio in your iCloud settings.
  • Decide what to export and where to send it.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • Turn sharing of crash reports with developers on or off in your device’s settings.

Where privacy law gives you rights over your information, you can email the address below with any question. Because your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud, you can view, change and delete it yourself at any time.

Children

Gear Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
