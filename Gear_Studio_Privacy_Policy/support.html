My designs don’t appear on my other device

Designs and custom library items sync through your private iCloud. Check that:

  • both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account;
  • iCloud is turned on for Gear Studio in your iCloud settings on both devices;
  • both devices are online, and Gear Studio has been opened on each.

Changes can take a few minutes to arrive. Settings and tolerance profiles are kept on each device and do not sync.

Gear Studio Pro

The free version includes spur gears, racks, STL export and three saved designs. Gear Studio Pro adds every gear type, 3MF, OBJ, DXF and assembly export, the PDF and CSV spec sheet, the Set Builder and physics estimates, core modifications such as hubs and lightening holes, custom materials and processes, and unlimited saved designs. Pro is a one-time purchase or an annual subscription, sold through the App Store. Your designs are never deleted if Pro ends.

Restoring a purchase

On a new device, or after reinstalling, open Settings in Gear Studio and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Managing or cancelling the subscription

The annual subscription renews automatically every year until you cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription in Gear Studio’s Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Exporting a design

Tap Export on a design or a gear set, choose whether to export nominal or compensated dimensions, pick a format, then share the file or save it to the Files app. STL is free; 3MF, OBJ, DXF, assembly export and the PDF or CSV spec sheet are part of Pro.

Deleting designs and data

Delete a design from the library to remove it from this device and, when sync is on, from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is stored on that device. To remove synced designs as well, delete them in the app first, or remove Gear Studio’s data in your iCloud storage settings. The privacy policy has the details.
