Unit Conversion

Support · Updated October 14, 2026

Unit Conversion by Threaded Realms converts between units across dozens of categories, from length and energy to data rates and cutting speeds. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17.6 or later, needs no account and works offline.

Sending feedback

Open Support in the app and tap Send feedback. Your mail app opens with a draft that already includes the app version and your device model, which help with troubleshooting. You can delete them before sending.

A conversion looks wrong

Send feedback with the category, the two units, the value you entered and the result you expected. Those details are enough to check the conversion.

No mail app on my device

If no mail app is set up, the app offers Copy address, so you can write from any email service.

Following Threaded Realms

News about the app is posted on Instagram (@com.threaded.realm) and X (@ThreadedRealms).
