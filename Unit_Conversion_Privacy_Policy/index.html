Unit Conversion

Privacy Policy · Updated October 14, 2026

This policy describes how the Unit Conversion app for iPhone and iPad, by Threaded Realms, handles information. Unit Conversion is published by Arison. In short: the app does its work on your device and collects nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. The values you type, the units you pick and the results are worked out on your device and kept only while the app is open. The app does not save them, sync them or send them anywhere.

Unit Conversion has no account and asks for no device permissions. It does not use your camera, photos, location, contacts or microphone, and it does not read your clipboard. It makes no network connections of its own.

Feedback email

If you choose Send feedback, the app opens your own mail app with a draft addressed to the developer. The draft includes the app version and your device model, which you can see and delete before sending. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

If you send it, the developer receives your message, the address your mail app sends from, and anything else you put in the email. They are used only to reply to you and to improve the app. If no mail app is set up, the app offers to copy the developer’s address so you can write from elsewhere.

Links to other sites

The Support screen links to Threaded Realms on Instagram and X. Tapping a link opens it in your browser or that service’s app. The app sends nothing with the link. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Third parties

Unit Conversion contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It does not sell or share your information.

If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may share crash reports for Unit Conversion with the developer. You can turn that off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. Apple’s handling is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps nothing between launches, so there is nothing in it to delete. Deleting the app removes it completely.

Feedback emails you send are kept in the developer’s mailbox for as long as they are useful for replying and improving the app. You can ask for your emails to be deleted by replying to the developer.

Your choices and rights

  • Decide whether to send feedback, and edit or remove the app version and device model from the draft first.
  • Turn sharing of crash reports with developers on or off in your device’s settings.

Where privacy law gives you rights over your information, such as access or deletion of emails you sent, use the contact details in the Contact section.

Children

Unit Conversion is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when new features arrive, the updated date on this page will change with it and a note here will say what changed. The current version is October 14, 2026.
